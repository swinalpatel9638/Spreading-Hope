Education Is the Difference

Education is the difference for millions of children around the globe. Whether trapped in poverty or stuck in exploitation, education offers hope of a better future. We've made it our mission to connect every child with their right to learn. Support our Education Programme today.

Our Education Services

Primary and Secondary Education for Children

We run primary and secondary classrooms across Northern Syria, giving displaced children a safe place to learn with qualified teachers, learning materials, and a structured curriculum so they can continue their education despite the disruption of conflict.

Adult Literacy Programs

Many adults missed years of schooling during the war. Our adult literacy programs help parents and young adults learn to read, write, and build numeracy skills, opening the door to better work and the ability to support their children's education.

Vocational Training for Employment

We offer vocational training in trades such as tailoring, carpentry, and technology, equipping young people with employable skills so they can find sustainable work, rebuild their independence, and contribute to their communities' recovery.

Dua's Story

"For a whole year after primary school, I didn't know if I'd ever sit in a classroom again. My parents kept asking around, but there was nowhere for me to go. When they told me a secondary school had opened in the camp, I couldn't stop smiling — I get to learn again, and I get to see my friends every day."

Stories like Dua's are only the beginning. With additional funding, we can hire more teachers, open more classrooms, and reach the children still waiting for a school to open near them. Every contribution helps turn a gap in a child's education into a fresh start.

Supporting Our Teachers

Behind every classroom is a teacher who chose to keep going. We train and support educators across Northern Syria — many displaced themselves — so they can deliver a stable, quality education even in the hardest conditions.

Our teacher programs provide training, learning materials, and ongoing mentorship, helping teachers create safe, encouraging spaces where children can recover, focus, and rediscover a love of learning.
