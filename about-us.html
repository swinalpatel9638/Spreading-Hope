Who We Are

Spreading Hope is a nonprofit organization continually striving to improve the lives of Syrians. Syrians have been victims of a dreadful civil war for over a decade. A peaceful uprising twelve years ago turned into a full-scale civil war that has left half a million people dead, devastated cities, and drawn in other countries. This NGO was started to help Syrians by collecting funds and allocating them to housing, food, health, and education.

Our Mission

Our goal at Spreading Hope is to give Syrians affected by years of civil violence the help and respect they deserve. We work tirelessly in the areas of housing, food, health, and education.

Our Programs

Every initiative aims to empower people, encourage healing, and build community resilience, giving individuals impacted by the crisis hope again and a brighter future.

How You Can Help

Spreading Hope is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering Syrians to rebuild their lives and their communities through sustainable, life-changing programs and services. Our comprehensive strategy includes education, training, job placement, economic empowerment, and research studies.

We also prioritize women's empowerment and child protection, working to ensure that all Syrians have the support and resources they need to thrive and succeed.
