How your donation is used

Every gift is split across the three areas where Syrian families need it most:

  • 40% Housing — transitional shelter and home repairs
  • 30% Education — school supplies, tuition support, and safe learning spaces
  • 20% Emergency relief — food, water, and urgent medical care
  • 10% Operations — keeping the organization running so more of every future dollar reaches families

🧵 As a thank-you, every donor receives a hand-stitched bracelet made by artisans in our partner workshops.
