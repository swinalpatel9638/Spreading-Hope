Working toward the peace and justice Syrians have long awaited.

Now in its fourteenth year, the crisis in Syria remains one of the world's largest displacement emergencies. More people than ever are sliding into deeper poverty, with 16.7 million in need of humanitarian assistance. A 2025 projection counts 7.2 million people displaced inside Syria and 6.2 million refugees sheltering in neighbouring countries.

Why donate?

Spreading Hope is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering Syrians and uplifting their lives by providing resources like shelter, food, education, emergency services, and much more.

With the help of your valuable donation, we will be able to accomplish our mission of helping Syrians.
