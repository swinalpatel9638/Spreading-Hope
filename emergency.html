What We Do

When disaster strikes, we move fast. Our emergency teams deliver urgent medical care, food, clean water, and shelter to Syrian families cut off by conflict and displacement, reaching the communities that need help most.

From mobile clinics to winter relief, our response is built to save lives in the moments that matter — and to keep supporting families long after the immediate crisis has passed.

Our Emergency Services

Emergency Medical Aid

Our mobile medical teams deliver urgent care to families cut off from hospitals, treating injuries, managing chronic conditions, and providing maternal and child healthcare, while supplying clinics with essential medicines and equipment.

Food and Water Distribution

We distribute food parcels and clean drinking water to displaced families facing severe shortages. Each parcel is designed to feed a family for a month, while water purification helps prevent the spread of disease in overcrowded camps.

Emergency Shelter and Safety

When families are forced to flee, we provide tents, blankets, and winter kits to protect them from harsh conditions, ensuring that the most vulnerable, especially children and the elderly, have a safe place to stay.

Rebuilding Health After Displacement

"Every clinic visit means one less thing I have to worry about while I'm raising my kids alone," says one mother our mobile team supports.

Families displaced by years of conflict often go months, even years, without access to a doctor. Chronic conditions that were once manageable become dangerous when medication runs out and specialists are hours away. Our mobile medical teams work to close that gap — screening patients, restocking essential medications, and connecting families with the ongoing care they'd otherwise have no way to reach. Every visit is a step back toward stability for a family that has already lost so much.

Providing Emergency Medical Care

Approximately 12.2 million people in Syria are in dire need of essential healthcare services. The protracted crisis has left a significant number of healthcare facilities in disarray — either outdated or severely damaged. As a result, a substantial portion of the population struggles to access the medical care they require.

We've undertaken the following key areas of action:

  • Emergency Care: We provide immediate, life-saving emergency care to victims of the ongoing conflict. Our medical teams work tirelessly to address injuries and traumas resulting from the crisis, ensuring that timely medical attention is accessible to those in need.
  • Chronic Illness: Beyond emergency services, we recognise the importance of ongoing care, offering regular healthcare support for individuals grappling with chronic illnesses.
  • Vaccination Programs: Preventative healthcare is vital. We administer vaccination programs to safeguard communities, particularly vulnerable groups such as children and the elderly, playing a crucial role in preventing outbreaks of infectious disease.
  • Mental Health Support: The psychological toll of conflict is immense. We provide essential mental health support to help individuals cope with the emotional challenges they face in the wake of the crisis.

We know that healthcare access can mean the difference between life and death for families already stretched thin by years of conflict. That's why we focus our medical support where it's needed most — mobile clinics that reach camps other services can't, and partnerships with local health facilities that keep essential care running for the communities around them. Every dollar donated toward our emergency medical programs goes directly to expanding that reach.

Yusuf's Story

Yusuf is eight years old and lives with his family in a displacement camp in Northwest Syria. Like many children in the camp, he spent months without access to basic checkups, and a treatable respiratory infection had gone unmanaged for weeks by the time our mobile clinic reached his community. Our team was able to treat him on the spot and enroll his family in ongoing care, so a routine illness never has to become an emergency again. His mother told our team, "Just knowing someone will come back means we're not facing this alone anymore."

Our commitment to healthcare is unwavering, and our impact is measured not only in numbers but in the lives transformed and the hope restored to those we serve in Syria.
